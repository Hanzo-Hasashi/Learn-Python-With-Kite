Account and Data Deletion

Use this page to request deletion of your Learn Python with Kite account and associated app data.

Request deletion

Send a deletion request from the email address associated with your Kite account:

Email a deletion request Open Kite account settings

What is deleted

After verification, Kite deletes the account, authentication sessions, profile, lesson and quiz progress, activity, achievements, notifications, saved settings and associated subscription/payment registrations that Kite is permitted to remove. Payment-provider and legal records may be retained only where required to resolve a transaction or meet a legal obligation.

Processing time

Requests are reviewed promptly. Kite may ask for identity verification so another person cannot delete your account. Confirmed deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

Partial deletion

To delete one category of data without deleting the account, email the support address and state which data should be changed or removed.
