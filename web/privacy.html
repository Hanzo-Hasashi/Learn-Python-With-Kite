This policy explains how Kite handles information in the app and website.

Information we process

Kite processes the account name and email address used to create or authenticate an account, learning progress, quiz and challenge activity, XP, streaks, achievements, preferences, notifications and subscription status. Information needed for a payment or subscription is processed by the relevant connected provider; Kite does not request or store a mobile-money PIN.

Code and technical information

Python code is sent to the configured execution runner only when you run or submit code. Hosting and operational providers may record ordinary request information such as IP address, requested path, time and response status for security and troubleshooting.

How information is used

Information is used to provide accounts and learning features, save progress, calculate rankings and achievements, deliver support, secure the service, verify subscriptions and meet legal obligations.

Service providers

Kite may use Supabase for authentication, a database for account and learning records, Google OAuth, an SMTP provider for account email, a separate runner for code execution and payment providers for payment processing. Provider terms and locations apply to information they process. Kite does not use advertising or analytics code in the current app.

Storage and retention

Account and learning records remain in the configured database while the account is used and until deleted or retained as required by law. Authentication cookies are HTTP-only where configured. Browser session and local storage may retain sign-in navigation state and interface preferences.

Your choices

You may update supported account details in the app. You may request account deletion or a copy of applicable account information through the account deletion page or by email.

Leaderboard visibility

The leaderboard may show the first word of an account name and XP score to signed-in users. It does not show account email addresses.

Contact

Privacy and deletion requests: learnwithkite.python@gmail.com

Kite may need to verify identity before fulfilling a request. We may retain limited information where required for legal, security or transaction records.
